Experience & Career Path

Data Scientist • Government R&D Project

Analysis and Forecasting of Traffic Data (2 Years)

I worked for two years as a Data Analyst and Data Scientist at DeepFeed Solutions on an extensive government research and development (R&D) project, contributing to the launch of the SIGESCANTT project (ANTT’s Integrated Contract Management System) — National Land Transportation Agency. My main responsibility was to build automated and dynamic analysis systems for highway parameters, mapping raw data to complex standards and guidelines (highway manuals and EHPs—Exploratory Highway Program).

Technical Impact: I developed data pipelines and interactive dashboards that allow users to select, filter, and cross-reference data across multiple years and highways (BRs). The system automatically generated reports on irregularities, heat maps, geographic maps, and maps of critical road segments, as well as temporal recurrence data for critical highway segments—facilitating the highway inspection process by transforming it into a systematic approach and eliminating the need for manual manipulation and verification of data spreadsheets. At the same time, I conducted machine learning experiments (predictive and regression models) based on the literature, with the aim of structuring a future phase of the project (RDT 2).
